# Sprint 4: Final Reflections

## Oleksandr Anyschenko

Having finally completed the project, I have gained a variety of valuable insights and learning experiences. The most important one of these to me is efficient team collaboration, the agile workflow, and the importance of early system design. When we were just getting started, it was confusing at first to figure out how to assign and use issues and pull requests, but with each sprint, I had grown appreciate the organization all these features from GitHub provide. Another important thing I learned was the usefulness of UML modeling and class / use case diagrams. I was initially introduced to this type of modeling in a prior database course that I had taken, however applying these concepts to software engineer truly showed me their effectiveness at effectively communicating system design, even to non-technical focused stakeholders. The most fun part for me was working together to break down a complex feature, such as implementing a scalable notification system, and watching every component work together to achieve the full functionality envisioned. While this was fun, at times these were also some of the most difficult parts of the project. Having to coordinate with all the team members to ensure everyone is on board with our implementation plan, and also having to resolve merge conflicts due to accidental reimplementations of the same components by multiple members can get quite challenging. Nonetheless, I believe that the skills that I am much better at now are: team collaboration, scalable system design, writing high quality code, and effective communication.
